Chronicles

History & Era Timeline 2006 – Present

Filter through Pierce The Veil's evolution from San Diego DIY roots to RIAA Platinum anthems and arena-filling modern triumphs.

2006 – 2007

San Diego Origins & A Flair for the Dramatic

Following the breakup of their previous band Before Today, brothers Vic and Mike Fuentes formed Pierce The Veil in San Diego and signed with Equal Vision Records. They recorded and self-produced their debut album A Flair for the Dramatic (June 2007) in Seattle with producer Casey Bates.

2007 – 2009

Tony Perry & Jaime Preciado Complete the Lineup

Shortly after recording their debut, lead guitarist Tony Perry and bassist Jaime Preciado officially joined the band, cementing the explosive four-piece chemistry showcased on the Vans Warped Tour and Tastes of Chaos.

2010 – 2011

Selfish Machines & Signing to Fearless Records

Produced with Mike Green, second album Selfish Machines (2010) delivered fan-favorite anthems like "Caraphernelia" (featuring Jeremy McKinnon) and "Bulletproof Love". In 2011, the band signed with Fearless Records.

2012 – 2015

Collide with the Sky & "King for a Day"

Released in July 2012, Collide with the Sky debuted at #12 on the Billboard 200 and produced "King for a Day" featuring Sleeping with Sirens' Kellin Quinn—later earning RIAA Platinum certification and sparking a massive viral resurgence a decade later.

2016 – 2019

Misadventures Debuts at #4 on Billboard 200

After meticulous studio sessions with Dan Korneff, Pierce The Veil released their fourth album Misadventures in May 2016, debuting at an impressive #4 on the US Billboard 200 led by "The Divine Zero", "Texas Is Forever", and "Circles".

2020 – Present

The Jaws of Life & Global Arena Era

In February 2023, PTV returned with their fifth studio album The Jaws of Life, featuring grunge-tinged lead single "Pass the Nirvana" and their first #1 Billboard Alternative Airplay hit "Emergency Contact", headlining packed amphitheaters and international tours.
